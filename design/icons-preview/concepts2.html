SALU · design preview II — no implementation

App-icon concepts · minimal directions

Six genuinely different geometries this time — each reduces the mark to fewer, quieter elements instead of recolouring the same loops. Sheet I (family marks + colourways) is at index.html.

Orbit 3 elements
One open ring, one soft triangle, one accent satellite where the ring breaks. The dot carries all the colour — everything else stays ink. Reads as "play in motion".
48 · 32 · 24 · 16 — dot drops at 24, ring+play survive
Lens vesica
The two loops simplified to plain thin rings; their overlap — the place both circles agree — becomes a horizontal lens that points right, like a play. The only colour is where the two meet.
48 · 32 · 24 — rings thicken as they shrink
Punch negative space
No drawn triangle at all — the play is a hole in a softly lit disc, so the wallpaper behind the icon would show through it in spirit. A faint accent arc at seven o'clock keeps the loop memory.
Script monoline S
The double loop unwound into one continuous S — SALU's initial — drawn as a single stroke with an accent terminal. The play nests quietly in the lower bowl. Calligraphic, signature-like, very quiet.
Frost fluent glass
The current mark, but treated like Windows 11 mica: loops as frosted glass at 30–45% ink, triangle crisp with a soft shadow, one accent glow behind. Familiar geometry, modern material.
Spectrum gradient line
A single open ring carrying the whole brand gradient — blue, teal, alive-green — around a plain white play. Colour as the line itself, not as a fill; the gap sits at the triangle's exit, like a signal path.
