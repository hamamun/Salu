SALU · design preview III — main app icon — no implementation

Main-icon concepts · Frost & Punch families, S marks, innovative player

Built on the two directions you picked: Frost (glass material) and Punch (negative space) — extended with S-letterform marks and two fully new player ideas. Sheets: I · II.

Frost II · Aura glass ring
One frosted ring instead of two loops, with a single bright "refraction" arc at ten o'clock — light catching glass. Crisp shadowed play floats inside an accent aura. Calmer than today's mark, same material.
40 · 28 · 20 — refraction arc drops first, glass ring survives
Frost III · Prism faceted glass
The tile itself is a mica pane; the play is a cut-glass prism with three facet tones — one shape, three lights. No ring at all. The most "Windows 11 native" of the set.
Punch II · Eclipse glowing hole
Punch, but the hole is lit from behind: the play-shaped opening glows accent-blue like light escaping an eclipse, with a matching rim arc at seven o'clock. Dark, cinematic, unmistakably a player.
40 · 28 · 20 — the glowing play carries every size
Punch III · Slot-S S of light
The S you asked for, carved as a negative channel through a dark disc — a gradient light layer beneath makes the letter glow edge-to-edge. SALU's initial as the whole icon; play lives in the app, letter on the tile.
40 · 28 · 20 — letterform stays legible tiny
S · Grotesk bold monoline
A confident single-stroke S, thick enough to own a taskbar slot, with one accent terminal dot — the same dot language as the family marks. Pure letterform, zero skeuomorphism.
S · Counter-play play in the bowl
The S's lower counter — the empty bowl of the letter — holds a small accent play. Letter and function in one glyph; at small sizes the accent dot-read still says "player".
Wave ring sound made circle
A circular waveform — twelve radial ticks swelling like audio — orbiting a quiet play. Two ticks take brand colour. Says "sound, playing" without a single loop from the old mark.
Beam motion play
Four rounded speed-lines whose lengths form a play silhouette — the triangle is implied, never drawn. The boldest, most modern cut; reads as media in motion even at 16 px.
